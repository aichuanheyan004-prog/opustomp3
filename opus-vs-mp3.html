Format comparison

Opus vs MP3

Opus and MP3 are both lossy audio formats, but they solve different problems. Opus is modern and efficient. MP3 is old, predictable, and widely supported.

Use Opus when

  • You want small files for speech, streaming, or real-time communication.
  • Your target apps already support Opus playback.
  • You are storing web-first recordings or chat audio.

Use MP3 when

  • You need broad compatibility with older players, editors, and upload forms.
  • You are sending files to people who may not know what Opus is.
  • A transcription or publishing tool rejects Opus input.

Should you convert Opus to MP3?

Convert only when you need compatibility. MP3 conversion creates a new lossy file, so it cannot improve the original sound. It simply makes the audio easier to use in more places.
